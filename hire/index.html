TypeScript performance expert for engineering teams

I help engineering teams make TypeScript fast again: slow type-checking, CI jobs that run out of memory, and the move to TypeScript 7. I measure what your types really cost, fix it without weakening type safety, and leave your team the tools to keep it fast.

What your team gets

Four ways to work together, each on your real codebase.

  • Service 1

    TypeScript performance audit

    I measure type-checking time and memory on your real codebase, find the types and files that cost the most, and deliver a prioritized list of fixes.

  • Service 2

    TypeScript 7 migration

    I measure TypeScript 6 against TypeScript 7 on your project, check memory in your CI, and plan a safe migration.

  • Service 3

    Type-checking budget in CI

    Budgets in your pipeline that catch a pull request before it slows the build down, based on the compiler's own counters, with a tolerance calibrated on your project.

  • Service 4

    Team training and workshops

    Advanced TypeScript and type performance, for Angular, React and Node.js teams.

How a project starts

We start with a short call about your codebase and what slows it down. The method is the same one behind the public experiments: measure, isolate one cause, fix it without weakening types, and publish the numbers to your team. See the merged fixes on Work, the experiments in the Lab, and the talks I give on Invite me to speak.
