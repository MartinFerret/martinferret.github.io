About Martin Ferret

Martin Ferret is a French TypeScript expert focused on type-checking performance, a contributor to Microsoft's TypeScript DOM types, and Lead Content Owner for the JavaScript and TypeScript certifications at Certificates.dev.

Work with me Invite me to speak

He works on how fast TypeScript checks types. He builds small experiments that isolate one cause of slowness, measures it on TypeScript 6 and 7, and publishes the result with the raw numbers in the TypeScript Performance Lab, including when the hypothesis turns out to be wrong.

When the cause sits in TypeScript’s own type definitions, he fixes it there: his pull requests to the DOM type definitions are merged into Microsoft’s TypeScript-DOM-lib-generator, listed on Work. He has spent seven years building large-scale enterprise applications with TypeScript and Angular.

He is the author of 30 articles on the Certificates.dev blog, listed on Writing. He co-hosted Devpreneur, a French-language podcast about entrepreneurship for developers, with Alain Chautard. He runs What's up TypeScript France, a free online meetup in French.

Method

How I work

The same loop for every slowdown, from the first measurement to the published result.

  1. Step 1

    Measure

    Run tsc with extended diagnostics on a real project and record check time, memory and instantiations.

  2. Step 2

    Isolate

    Shrink the slowdown to one cause in a small experiment, with a safety file proving the types stay as strict.

  3. Step 3

    Fix upstream

    When the cause lives in TypeScript’s own types, open a pull request there, not a workaround.

  4. Step 4

    Publish

    Ship the raw numbers and the machine they ran on, whatever the verdict.
